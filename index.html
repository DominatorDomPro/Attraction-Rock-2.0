The Mighty "Munros"

In Scotland, mountains over 3,000 feet are called Munros. There are 282 of them, and hiking all of them is a legendary feat called "Munro Bagging." Perfect team building idea?

☁️

Older Than the Alps

The Scottish Highlands are incredibly ancient. Formed hundreds of millions of years ago, these rocky peaks were once as tall as the modern Himalayas before eons of ice and weather carved them down.

🦕

The Great Glen

The Highlands are literally split in half by a massive geological fault line called the Great Glen, which contains some of Scotland's deepest, most romantic, and myth-filled lochs.

Famous Scottish Runaway Romance

You aren't the first young couples to use the Scottish landscape to bypass the rules.

The Rebels of Gretna Green

In 1754, England passed a law preventing couples under 21 from marrying without parental consent. Scotland didn't implement the rule. The result? Thousands of young, desperate English couples sprinted across the border to the tiny Scottish village of **Gretna Green**. They would marry instantly over a blacksmith's anvil, cementing Scotland's legacy as the ultimate escape haven for young love defying constraints.

The "Annie Laurie" Confession

One of Scotland's most famous romantic ballads describes young William Douglas writing poetry to Annie Laurie in the late 1600s amid political and familial turmoil. Despite intense cultural pressures forcing them apart, their unspoken devotion amidst the rolling hills became a timeless symbol of enduring Scottish affection.
